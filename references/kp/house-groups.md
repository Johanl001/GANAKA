# KP House Groups — Starter List

Each event is tied to a specific group of houses. An event is judged by the cuspal sub-lords of all houses in the group collectively. All must support for a strong "yes".

| Event | Houses | Rationale |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Marriage | 2, 7, 11 | 2 = family, 7 = spouse/partnership, 11 = gains/fulfillment of desire |
| Job and career | 2, 6, 10, 11 | 2 = income, 6 = service/employment, 10 = profession/career, 11 = achievement/gains |
| Higher education | 4, 9, 11 | 4 = learning/education, 9 = higher studies/dharma, 11 = fulfillment |
| Foreign travel or settlement | 3, 9, 12 | 3 = short journeys, 9 = long-distance/foreign, 12 = settlement abroad/loss of homeland |
| Children | 2, 5, 11 | 2 = family addition, 5 = progeny, 11 = fulfillment |

**Rule:** For an event, examine the sub-lord of each cusp in the group. If all cuspal sub-lords signify the event houses (and none signify the denying houses), the event is supported.

**Denying houses by event:**
- Marriage: 1, 6, 10
- Job/career: 12 (loss of position)
- Higher education: 12 (loss of learning)
- Foreign travel/settlement: 1 (obstruction of going)
- Children: 12 (loss of progeny)
